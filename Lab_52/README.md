# Lab_52 - STL Algorithms: Peaches

Build with `g++ -std=c++17 -Wall peaches.cpp -o peaches`. Try basket sizes 8, 3, 1 and 0.

Think about what `remove_if` and `remove_copy_if` return, and why `erase` needs two iterators. The "every 3rd peach" loop deserves a careful look when the peck has fewer than three peaches.
